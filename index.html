About Edge Ark

A boutique firm built around personal service.

Edge Ark is 100% Australian owned and managed by its principal owners. Through its alliances, the firm offers a broad range of trading opportunities, whether you are an experienced trader or just getting started.

The mission is simple: investment solutions for serious investors who value personal service. Clients include individuals, corporates, high net worth clients, overseas nationals and major financial institutions, and trust and integrity sit at the centre of every relationship.

Sustainable investing uses environmental, social and governance (ESG) considerations as a key driver for selecting investments, combined with traditional financial analysis. Environmental covers things like carbon emissions, waste and recycling, and supply chain management. Social covers diversity, health and safety, and human rights. Governance covers board structure and size, transparency and ownership structure.
